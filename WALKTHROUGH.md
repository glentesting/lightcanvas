# How to Use LightCanvas

## Starting the app

On your Desktop there is an icon called **Start LightCanvas**. Double-click it.

A black window will open with some text in it. **Leave that black window
alone** — it is the engine that runs the app. About fifteen seconds later,
your web browser will open by itself and show LightCanvas.

When you are done for the day, close the black window. That turns the app
off. Next time, just double-click the icon again.

## What you'll see first

A page called **Projects** with one item on it: **My Christmas Show 2026**.
That is your show. Click **Open**.

## Your show

Now you are looking at the main screen. Three parts:

- **Left side — "Your Lights."** A list of every light piece in your yard:
  your 8 mini trees, 8 arches, 40 yard stakes, the 4 singing faces, and the
  plug-in lights for the roof and windows. All 84 of them, already set up
  with the right number of bulbs and the right plugs on your controller
  boxes. You never have to type any of that. Click any name in the list to
  see its details on the right side.

- **Middle — the picture.** For now it shows a stand-in cartoon house.
  Once you add a photo of your real house (next section), this becomes your
  house at night with your actual lights on it.

- **Bottom — the play bar.** There is already a finished show in here, set
  to the song "Wreaths Like Horseshoes." **Click the round blue ▶ button.**
  The music plays and the lights on the picture dance along with it. Click
  the same button to pause.

## Putting your own house in the picture

1. Take a photo of the front of your house. Straight on, the whole house in
   the frame, daytime is fine. Get it onto this computer (email it to
   yourself, or plug in your phone).
2. In LightCanvas, click **Edit Layout** (bottom-right of the picture).
3. Click **Upload Photo** at the top and pick your photo.
4. Your house appears. Now drag each light piece to where it really is:
   grab a mini tree with your mouse and pull it to where that tree sits in
   your yard. The trees, arches, and stakes keep their shape while you drag.
   Everything you move stays where you put it — even after you close the
   app.
5. Click **Back to Designer** (top-left) to return to the main screen. The
   picture is now your house.

Don't worry about getting placement perfect. You can come back and nudge
things any time.

## Using your own song

Your songs are in your LightCanvas music folder. To change the song:

1. On the main screen, look at the left side under **Song**.
2. Click the little circular-arrows button next to the song name.
3. Tick the box that says you have the right to use the music, then pick
   your song file.
4. The app uploads it and then **listens to it to find the beat. The screen
   will sit still for a minute or two while it listens — that is normal.
   Don't close anything.** When the song name shows up with its length, it's
   done.

## Making a new show

On the left side, under **Make a Show**, click **Create my light show**.

A panel opens on the right. Pick a style (like "Classic Holiday"), pick
colors, pick how wild you want it, then click **Generate from Music**. The
computer listens to your song and writes a show for all 84 of your light
pieces — every flash lands on the beat. It takes a minute or two and you
can watch its progress.

When it finishes, press ▶ to watch. Don't like it? Click one of the
suggestions under "Refine" (like "Make it more energetic") and it redoes
the show. You can also click **Undo** to throw it away. Redo it as many
times as you want — nothing you do here can break anything.

## Getting the show onto your lights

When you have a show you love:

1. Click **Export** at the top of the main screen.
2. Choose **Light-O-Rama S6** (already selected) and click **Next**.
3. It asks for one of your purchased Light-O-Rama sequence files — pick any
   of them (they're the files that came with the sequences you bought).
4. The app matches all 84 of your light pieces automatically — you'll see
   "84 of 84 matched." Tick the "I've reviewed" box and click **Export**.
5. A file downloads. That file is your show, ready for the Light-O-Rama
   software to put on the memory card that goes in your show player box.

## If something looks wrong

- **Browser didn't open?** Wait 30 seconds, then open your browser yourself
  and go to: `localhost:3000`
- **Black window closed by accident?** Nothing is lost. Double-click
  Start LightCanvas again.
- **The picture froze?** If music is playing but the picture stopped, click
  pause, then play again. If the whole page looks stuck, press F5 on your
  keyboard (that reloads the page). Your work is saved automatically every
  few seconds, so reloading never loses anything.
